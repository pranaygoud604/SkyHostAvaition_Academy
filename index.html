Chapter 07 · Questions answered

Before you apply.

What is the minimum eligibility for the CPL program?
Class 12 with Physics & Mathematics (minimum 50% aggregate), age 17–32, and a DGCA Class-2 medical clearance. We facilitate the medical for shortlisted candidates at our partner facility.
Are scholarships available?
Yes — merit, sport, and need-based scholarships covering up to 35% of program fees. Reviewed individually by the founder's office within seven days of enrolment.
Do you provide placement guarantees?
We guarantee minimum-three airline interviews for every cadet who clears the program with first-attempt module passes. Our historical first-attempt placement rate is 96%.
Where is the campus located?
Our main campus is in HAL II Stage, Bengaluru, with the flight academy operating out of Jakkur Aerodrome and our 737 NG simulator bay at the Devanahalli training centre.
Can I tour the campus before applying?
Always. Submit the form above with your preferred date and our admissions chief will host you personally — including a guided walkthrough of the simulator bay and a sit-down with the program director.
